Privacy Policy — draft

Nexus may handle account usernames, email addresses, password hashes, profile pictures, session cookies, and temporary game information such as room codes, player names, moves, guesses, answers, and chat. Hosting providers may also process basic technical logs.

Information is used to operate accounts and games, maintain security, prevent abuse, and keep the service working. Account records include usernames, email addresses, password hashes, account creation time, profile image data, online-visibility settings, email-verification state, and records of Terms acceptance and Privacy Policy acknowledgement. The service also processes session identifiers and temporary game information such as room codes, player names, moves, guesses, answers, and chat messages. The Pokémon game requests move data directly from PokéAPI from the player’s browser; that provider may receive the browser request and network information such as an IP address.

The service uses a first-party HttpOnly session cookie for signed-in sessions. Game pages use browser local storage to keep a room-resume token, room code, game type, and player name so a player can reconnect after refreshing. These values are used for session continuity and are not an analytics profile. Do not store passwords, email addresses, or authentication session cookies in local storage.

Information is used to operate accounts and games, maintain security, prevent abuse, and keep the service working. Other players should only receive the profile and game information needed for play. Email addresses, password hashes, session tokens, and private account details must not be exposed to other players.

Users can control whether friends see their online status and can request account deletion. Some data may remain in backups temporarily. The final policy must identify actual providers, retention periods, contact details, and any applicable privacy rights. Do not assume that this draft by itself establishes compliance with children's privacy laws.

Before publication: the operator must add its legal identity and a monitored privacy contact; confirm the actual hosting/database providers and retention periods; document backup and log retention; confirm whether Resend is configured for verification email; and review applicable youth privacy requirements. This page is not final until those facts and the applicable legal requirements have been checked.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy — draft

Nexus currently uses a first-party nexus_session cookie to keep a signed-in account session active. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and is Secure in production. Its configured lifetime is seven days. This cookie is needed for account authentication.

The game pages also use browser local storage under the key nexusSession to save a temporary room-resume token, room code, game type, and player name. This supports reconnecting to a game after a refresh. Clearing this browser storage may prevent automatic room rejoining.

The source reviewed for this draft did not show an advertising or analytics cookie or a third-party cookie consent platform. The Pokémon game loads move names directly from PokéAPI in the browser. The actual live browser requests, hosting configuration, and any future integrations must be checked before this statement is relied upon. If nonessential tracking is introduced, update this policy and implement any consent controls required by applicable law before it runs.

Before publication: verify cookie and storage behavior in a clean production browser, identify any provider-set cookies, confirm the operator contact and applicable jurisdictions, and update this page whenever tracking technologies change.

Terms of Service — draft

Use a truthful username, protect your account, and do not access someone else's account without permission. Do not use Nexus to harass, threaten, bully, impersonate, share another person's private information, disrupt games, exploit bugs, or violate applicable law.

Nexus may experience bugs, downtime, or changes. Accounts may be restricted for serious or repeated abuse, subject to a clear process that still needs to be finalized. Users retain rights they have in content they upload and grant only the permission needed to operate the service.

Cosmetics and Nexus Plus are planned future features and are not being offered by this draft. Final terms must describe the operator, age requirements, support contact, governing law, moderation process, account actions, and effective date.

Community Guidelines — draft

  • Treat other players respectfully and keep names, profile pictures, and chat suitable for a general audience.
  • Do not threaten, bully, harass, discriminate against, or repeatedly target another player.
  • Do not share anyone's address, school, contact details, passwords, or private messages without permission.
  • Do not impersonate players or staff, spam chat, disrupt rooms, or exploit bugs.
  • Only upload pictures and content you have the right to use.

Reporting and moderation workflows must be implemented and verified before Nexus promises a particular review process or response time.

Purchases & Refunds — draft for future paid features

Nexus may offer optional cosmetic items and a Nexus Plus membership in the future. Purchases and subscriptions should remain disabled until the operator, payment provider, prices, taxes, customer support, cancellation flow, refund process, and applicable legal requirements are ready.

Before a paid checkout is enabled, users must be shown the total price, whether a charge recurs, renewal timing, cancellation steps, what the purchase includes, and applicable refund terms. Nothing in a future policy should claim to remove rights that applicable law does not allow a business to waive.
