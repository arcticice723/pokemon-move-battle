## 1. About Nexus

Nexus is a multiplayer game hub. Features may change as the service is developed. The final terms must accurately describe the live service and the operator.

## 2. Accounts

Keep account information accurate, choose a username that does not impersonate another person, and protect your sign-in details. Do not use another person's account without permission. Contact support if you believe someone has accessed your account.

The operator should not promise account recovery or email verification until those features are available.

## 3. Fair and respectful use

You may not use Nexus to threaten, harass, bully, stalk, impersonate, share another person's private information, exploit bugs, disrupt rooms, evade safety measures, or violate applicable law. Do not upload profile images or content that you do not have the right to use or that violates the community rules.

## 4. Safety and moderation

Nexus may provide blocking, reporting, and moderation tools. Reports may be reviewed to protect users and the service. The final terms must explain the actual moderation process, possible account restrictions, and how users can appeal significant enforcement decisions. Do not promise response times or 24/7 moderation unless those are actually provided.

## 5. User content

You retain any rights you have in content you upload. You give Nexus only the limited permission needed to store, display, and transmit that content as part of the service. The final version should clarify handling of reported content, backups, and content remaining in other players' screenshots or records.

## 6. Service availability

Nexus may experience downtime, bugs, or changes. The operator should make reasonable efforts to maintain the service but must not promise uninterrupted availability. Any suspension or discontinuation terms must be reviewed for fairness and local law.

## 7. Paid features (future)

Cosmetics and Nexus Plus are planned, not currently promised features. Before sales begin, publish clear prices, what each purchase includes, whether a subscription renews, how to cancel, refund terms, and any platform/payment-provider conditions. Do not charge users based on this draft.

## 8. Privacy

Use of Nexus is also covered by the final Privacy Policy. The policy must accurately describe what data is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

## 9. Account suspension and deletion

The final terms should explain when accounts may be restricted for safety, abuse, fraud, or serious violations. Users should have a clear account-deletion process. Any retention after deletion must be disclosed and justified.

## 10. Disclaimers and limits

Any disclaimer or limitation of liability must be tailored to applicable law. Do not publish broad language claiming to remove rights that users cannot legally waive.

## 11. Changes and contact

The final terms must explain how updates are communicated, when they take effect, and how users can contact the operator.
