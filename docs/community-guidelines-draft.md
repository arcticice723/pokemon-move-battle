# Nexus Community Guidelines — Draft

**Status: DRAFT — review and publish only after moderation tools and reporting workflows are ready.**

Nexus should be a place to play games without being harassed or exposed to unwanted contact.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other players respectfully.
- Keep usernames, profile pictures, room chat, and uploaded content appropriate for a general audience.
- Do not threaten, bully, harass, discriminate against, or repeatedly target another player.
- Do not share anyone's private information, including their real name, address, school, contact details, passwords, or private messages without permission.
- Do not impersonate another player or Nexus staff.
- Do not spam, flood chats, exploit bugs, cheat, or intentionally disrupt games.
- Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use or that violates other people's rights.
- Do not use Nexus to solicit money, deceive players, or send harmful links.

## Safety tools

When implemented, users should be able to block a player, report a specific concern, and control who can contact or invite them. Blocking should stop direct requests and invitations where technically possible. Reports should be reviewed using a documented process.

## Possible actions

Depending on severity and repeated behavior, Nexus may remove content, limit features, temporarily suspend accounts, or permanently close accounts. Serious threats or credible safety concerns may require additional steps consistent with applicable law.
