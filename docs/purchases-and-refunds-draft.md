# Nexus Purchases and Refunds — Draft for Future Paid Features

**Status: DRAFT — NOT ACTIVE. Nexus should not accept purchases or subscriptions until a payment provider, operator, price list, customer support contact, and final policy are in place.**

## Planned purchase types

Nexus may offer optional cosmetic items such as profile frames, themes, and badges, and may later offer a Nexus Plus membership. Core games, basic friend features, and essential privacy and safety controls should remain free.

## Before a purchase

The checkout must clearly show:
- The item or membership being purchased.
- The total price and currency.
- Whether the payment is one-time or recurring.
- The membership renewal schedule, if applicable.
- How to cancel a subscription and when cancellation takes effect.
- Any important restrictions or expiration dates.

Do not use preselected paid add-ons or misleading purchase buttons. Do not sell randomized paid loot boxes in the initial business plan.

## Refunds and consumer rights

The final policy must explain how to request help or a refund, what information is needed, and any lawful limits. Nothing in the policy should claim to remove rights that applicable law does not allow a business to waive. Payment-provider rules and mandatory local consumer laws may affect outcomes.

## Subscriptions

If Nexus Plus launches, users must be told clearly if it renews automatically. Cancellation should be easy to find and should not require an unreasonable process. The final policy must describe whether benefits continue through the paid period after cancellation.

## Account deletion

The operator must decide how deleting an account affects purchased cosmetics, active subscriptions, legally required transaction records, and refunds. Explain this before taking payment. Account deletion must not be represented as automatically canceling a subscription managed directly by a third-party payment provider unless that is actually implemented.

- [ ] Choose a payment provider and verify its eligibility and contract requirements.
- [ ] Complete business, tax, and adult-supervision steps as applicable.
- [ ] Set prices and implement secure, provider-hosted checkout.
- [ ] Implement ownership records, cancellation, support, and refund workflows.
- [ ] Review consumer, subscription, privacy, and tax requirements.
- [ ] Replace this draft with a reviewed final policy before enabling payments.
